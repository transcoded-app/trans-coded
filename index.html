Trans Coded

Pop in a disc. Get a tidy media library.

What it does

Trans Coded rips your Blu-rays and DVDs, works out what's on them, names everything properly and files it into your media library. Movies, shows, extras, even discs full of shorts. You watch the progress from a little dashboard in your browser.

A MacA Blu-ray or DVD driveSome discs

No accounts, keys or sign-ups. It's free, and the install takes about three minutes.

Install

Open Terminal, paste this line and press Return.

curl -fsSL https://github.com/transcoded-app/trans-coded/releases/latest/download/get-trans-coded.sh | bash

What happens next

  1. The installer sets things upIt asks a few questions, like where your movies and shows go. macOS is careful, so expect a few pop-ups too, each explained as it comes. Three notices that "background items" were added are normal: that's Trans Coded's helpers.
  2. The dashboard opensKeep it open in your browser. That's where you'll see what's happening, with posters and all. You can open it on your phone too, at the address the installer shows. No sound? Click anywhere on the page.
  3. Pop in a discTrans Coded takes it from there and asks you if it's unsure about anything.

Tip: keep your media folder on an external drive or in its own folder, not in Documents, Desktop or Downloads. macOS guards those and can block the rips.

Changed your mind?

This takes Trans Coded off your Mac. Your movies, shows and rips stay where they are, and everything else goes to the Trash, not deleted.

curl -fsSL https://github.com/transcoded-app/trans-coded/releases/latest/download/uninstall.sh | bash
